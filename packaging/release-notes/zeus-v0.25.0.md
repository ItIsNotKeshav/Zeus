Zeus 0.25.0 brings the recent look-and-feel work together and adds a choice of update channel.

## New

- **Update channel.** In Settings, About, pick Stable (tested releases only) or Beta (early builds first). The choice is remembered, and the next check follows it.
- **Doodle wallpapers.** Pick a doodle style, then set its intensity and colour; the wallpaper also shows through the composer.
- **Frosted composer and go-to-bottom button**, with reaction chips on the far side of the bubble and a remembered picker mode.
- **Typing dots** now appear inside an incoming bubble, and the gap above the composer is tighter.
- **Media previews** in the composer follow the shape of the photo or video you are sending.

## Fixed

- The update window no longer keeps an offer from a channel you have switched away from.
