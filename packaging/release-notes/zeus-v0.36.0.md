Zeus 0.36.0 is lighter and calmer: it uses much less memory, runs smoothly on high refresh rate screens, and Halloween has been packed away until next year.

## New

- **A built-in benchmark.** Settings > About can run a two-minute test of smoothness, memory, CPU and GPU with sample chats, and save or share the results. Your own chats are never used.
- **Animated stickers play on their own** in a chat while they are on screen, and loop.
- **Doodle density.** A new slider sets how many doodles fill the wallpaper, and the doodle sliders now read like the Glass slider, with names at both ends.
- **Zeus opens on its welcome page** instead of the last chat you had open.
- **Roomier message field.** It sits evenly inside the chat, with the chat's corners rounded to match it, and the text starts a little further from the emoji button.

## Fixed

- **Much less memory.** Pictures and stickers you have scrolled past are let go sooner, and a window left alone or minimized keeps only what it shows. In our test, peak memory dropped from about 490 MB to under 390 MB, and idle memory from about 430 MB to about 125 MB.
- **Chats open whole**, with their newest messages already in place, instead of blank for a moment.
- The react and reply buttons fade in and out gently and no longer shift messages while you hover.
- The Glass slider now changes only the frosted header, message field and menus, so the chat and chat list keep their colour across the whole slider.
- The chat list no longer casts a shadow across the chat beside it, and the dark band under message bubbles is gone.
- Short messages show their whole time underneath instead of cutting it off, and a photo no longer shows its time twice.
- The composer appears the moment an admin opens a group to everyone.
- Favourite chats no longer retry syncing in an endless loop.
- Specially crafted text and pictures can no longer slow down or crash Zeus.
- Scrolling on macOS is smoother.
- Spacing in the doodle settings is more even.

## Removed

- The Halloween look (orange theme, night wallpaper, pumpkin icons, spooky sounds and surprises). If you had it on, Zeus switches back to the classic doodles and sounds.
