Zeus 0.43.0 gives the chat list its own way around, moves Settings into a cleaner home, and makes everyday chats feel brighter and calmer.

## New

- **A chat list that gets out of your way.** A floating tab bar sits at its foot: Chats, Calls, your avatar and Settings. The title is a menu for Chats, Starred, Archived and Locked chats. When the list is hidden, a slim rail takes over with bigger buttons and clear groups.
- **A tab bar with a little life.** Hover to see the chat bubble wobble, the phone ring, the gear turn and your avatar grow. Press a button and it sinks, then springs back.
- **Settings, freshly arranged.** Your account sits in a card under the search bar and opens the account switcher. Pages appear beside their categories, each group of rows on its own card, with no Back arrow crowding the title.
- **Appearance in one place.** See your theme on a real chat preview, scroll through theme tiles, and pick an accent from a row of colours or the colour wheel. Mode, Glass and wallpaper tint sit on the card below. There is also an app icon picker.
- **Your accent goes with every theme.** Custom themes keep it too. Tap the chosen dot on the wheel to return to the theme's own colour.
- **Previews that tell you what is waiting.** Chat rows now lead with a small glyph for the kind of message: photo, video, GIF, voice, audio, document, sticker, location, contact, poll or call. A missed call shows in red.
- **Voice notes, restyled.** Voice messages have a cleaner look, and you can drag to seek through them. Composer buttons now sit outside the message field.
- **Times, only when you want them.** Times appear on hover and in Message info. Only your newest message keeps its time underneath, with a centred line marking longer gaps. "Sent", "Delivered" and "Read" wait a moment under a first message, then step aside when you reply.
- **Attachments fly into place.** A pasted or attached file shows at the size it will be sent, then flies into its bubble with its caption. Albums stay together as one grid while pictures arrive.
- **The info card slides in.** It covers the right side of the chat instead of widening the window.
- **A warmer WhatsApp look.** Its built-in theme borrows the Mac app's colours: greener buttons, readable text, softer avatars and doodles. Light themes are brighter, wallpaper tint keeps the wallpaper's own lightness, and the beige chat background is warmer.
- **Doodles, packed like WhatsApp's.** Mixed sizes fill each tile neatly, with thinner lines and a colour of their own in Settings.
- **A nicer Mac installer.** The disk image opens with only Zeus and Applications on a blue backdrop and an arrow to drag. Licence files live inside the app.
- **Call history, easier to scan.** Call entries use a smaller title and a larger detail line.

## Fixed

- **Less memory on Mac.** After a minute away, Zeus lets go of its graphics memory and keeps a soft last frame. Typing, a call or a new message in view brings it straight back.
- **Opening photos no longer leaves a memory peak.** Pictures decode at the size you see, and a bigger one only when you zoom in.
- **The sticker picker stays light.** Stickers decode at the size they are drawn, only nearby rows are laid out, and everything is freed when the picker closes.
- **Chat rows keep their line.** A faint hairline parts rows and call entries from their text to the edge, and it hides when a row is selected.
- **Small things, tidied.** Menus, popups, dialogs and dragged cards cast tighter shadows. Fields, chips and the composer get a fine outline. Reply cards size to their quote instead of a fixed width.
