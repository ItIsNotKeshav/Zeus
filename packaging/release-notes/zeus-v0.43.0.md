Zeus 0.43.0 feels calmer and easier to use. Find your way around with a new bar at the foot of your chats, enjoy a simpler Settings, and see your conversations in a brighter, more personal light.

## New

- **A better way around.** A small bar at the bottom of your chat list takes you to Chats, Calls, your profile and Settings. Your profile opens all your accounts in one place.
- **Every view, one tap away.** The title at the top opens Chats, Starred, Archived and Locked chats. When the list is tucked away, a slim rail keeps the same places close at hand.
- **A little life in the bar.** Icons respond when you hover, and a press sinks gently and springs back.
- **Settings, made simple.** Your account sits up front, and each group of options has its own card. The page you open appears beside the categories, without a Back arrow getting in the way.
- **Make it yours, in one place.** Preview a theme on a real chat, scroll through themes, and choose an accent from a row of colours or the colour wheel. Mode, Glass and wallpaper tint live on the card below. You can also pick a new app icon.
- **Your accent, everywhere.** The colour you choose now works with every theme, including themes you add yourself. Tap the chosen colour again to go back to the theme's own.
- **Know what is waiting.** Each chat row shows a small sign for the kind of message inside: a photo, video, voice note, document and more. Missed calls stand out in red.
- **Voice notes, refreshed.** They have a cleaner look, and you can drag to move through them.
- **Times, only when you need them.** Your newest message keeps its time. Older ones show theirs when you hover or open Message info. Delivery updates wait quietly under a first message, then step aside once you reply.
- **Attachments settle in.** A file you paste or attach appears at the size it will send, then moves neatly into the chat with its caption.
- **More room to read.** The info panel slides over the chat instead of making the window wider.
- **A warmer look.** WhatsApp's built-in theme borrows the Mac app's colours. Light themes are brighter, wallpaper tint keeps the wallpaper's own brightness, and doodles look tidier and feel more familiar.
- **A friendlier Mac installer.** The disk image opens with just Zeus and Applications and an arrow to drag.

## Fixed

- **Less memory on a Mac.** When Zeus sits idle, it lets go of memory and keeps a soft last frame. A key press, a call or a new message brings it straight back.
- **Opening pictures and stickers stays light.** They no longer leave extra memory behind after you close them.
- **Small things, tidied.** Menus, popups and cards cast softer shadows. Reply cards fit the words they quote instead of taking up a fixed space.
