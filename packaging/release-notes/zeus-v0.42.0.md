Zeus 0.42.0 brings a redesigned Appearance page, chat bubbles that can do much more, drawing on photos before you send them, and HD photos and videos.

## New

- **A new Appearance page.** Settings > Appearance has a live preview, a colour wheel for your own accent, and a Motion setting: Default, or Fast for animations that run in 70% of the time.
- **Settings in seven groups**, with less helper text.
- **Draw on a photo before sending it.** The picture editor gains a pen, a highlighter, and emoji or stickers you can move and resize, plus Undo. View once is in its top bar.
- **Drag and drop between chats.** Carry a message or picture to a chat bubble, the other side of split view, or the media panel.
- **Chat bubbles do more.** Bubbles show photos and videos, glow while their chat has unread messages, and stay on their own desktop. You can drag an open card by its header. The reply field matches the chat's own, with emoji shortcodes, an emoji picker and multi-line messages. Attachments show as previews on glass, and you can paste a picture into a bubble. Clicking a photo opens it in the main viewer.
- **Bubble new messages.** Turn this on in a chat's info, and a new message from that chat brings up its bubble while Zeus is in the background. Up to three chats can be bubbles at once.
- **HD photos and videos.** Media a phone sends in HD now plays in HD, and videos you send carry a sharp preview picture instead of a pixelated one.
- **Reply to view-once media**, and a reply shows the photo it quotes.
- **The composer's strips open out of it.** Reply, edit, attachments and link previews rise out of the message field on a spring and sink back when they're sent. The chat has a flatter look, without shadows round the field and strips.
- **Rubber-band overscroll** in chats.
- **A user guide** for people new to Zeus.

## Fixed

- **The main chats come first after a long time away.** History sync files your main chats before archived ones, so the list and sending aren't held up by big archived groups.
- **Delivery labels say it once.** "Delivered" or "Read" shows under your latest message only. It folds away from the previous message as soon as you send a new one.
- Messages that tag a community group show up instead of saying they're unsupported.
- A wide picture added to the message field shows whole instead of squeezed.
- Network requests time out instead of waiting forever.
- Drag and drop no longer freezes the app.
- A bubble's photos stay sharp while the main window is minimized.
