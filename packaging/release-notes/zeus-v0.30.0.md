Zeus 0.30.0 is a big one: Halloween arrives, touchpad swipes come to every platform, and pictures sent together now travel as one album.

## New

- **Halloween.** An orange theme over any theme and accent, a night wallpaper, pumpkin and ghost icons, spooky doodles and sounds, and the odd ghost, skeleton or candy drifting by. Each part can be switched on its own in Settings, Halloween, or the whole look turned off.
- **Touchpad swipes.** Swipe a chat with two fingers to archive, pin, mute, favourite or mark it read, swipe a message to reply to it, and swipe the open chat away. Settings chooses what each swipe does, and a swiped change to a chat offers Undo in a toast.
- **Albums.** Pictures and clips chosen together are sent as one album, with one download button and save options in the right-click menu.
- **Edit messages in place:** double-click your own text to edit it in its bubble.
- **Reactions popup** lists who reacted with room for three people, and its chips add or remove your own reaction.
- **Mute submenu** in a chat's right-click menu, and a cooldown that quiets notifications during a flood of messages.
- **Frosted chat list header**, and the chat search now finds names with letters left out, below the exact matches.
- **Eased menus and popups** that open and close smoothly.
- **Drafts** keep the caret at the end when you come back, and a chat with a draft sorts as if you had just written in it.
- Updates are checked every two hours.

## Fixed

- Zeus no longer keeps redrawing the window while it sits idle with a menu closed, which saves battery.
- Tab moves from a text field to the next one, so you can type a new lock code and then its confirmation.
- A forwarded photo without a caption has an even rim on both sides.
- The maximized window's title bar on Windows is as tall as the floating one.
- Toasts now sit at the bottom left, stacked above the status banner, instead of covering the chat.
- The reply preview above the composer is back, with even padding on a reply quote.
- The time stays visible on narrow pictures, and the typing row lines up with messages.
- The search box's focus ring is quieter.
