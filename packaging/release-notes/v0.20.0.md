ZapFast 0.20.0 makes and answers WhatsApp calls: voice and video, one-to-one and, as an experiment, in groups. It rings for an incoming call, and a new Calls page lists every call. Calls are new and video calls are in alpha, so please try them, and if something breaks, point your agents at it: pull requests are very welcome. A rail beside the chat list now holds Chats, Calls, Starred and Settings. Sound and recording run on a new audio layer that talks to PipeWire directly on Linux. This release also brings group invite links and group management, starred and pinned messages, a full-window photo and video viewer, chat info in a side panel, sound in WhatsApp's videos, deletions that sync with your phone, and dozens of fixes from more than forty contributors. The prebuilt Linux packages need Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13 or newer: Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12 are not supported.

## New

- **Voice calls.** The phone button in a chat's header calls that person, and an incoming call takes over the window with Accept and Decline, raises a notification and rings, even with the window in the tray. You hear a ringback tone while their phone rings. During a call you can mute, switch the microphone or speaker, and put the call screen aside to keep chatting while it runs. A muted, archived or locked chat's call stays as quiet as its messages. By @crmne, building on the work of @alitura1. (#220)
- **Video calls, in alpha.** The camera button starts a video call, turns your camera off and on, or switches a voice call to video from either side. A computer without a camera still takes part and sees the other side. Your picture goes out at 25 frames a second, up to 720p, and follows the connection: it steps down rather than stalling when the other side receives less. Each picture is sent with repair packets, as WhatsApp's own apps send them, so a lost packet is rebuilt on the other side instead of freezing the picture until the next full one. An iPhone relies on them, and with them calls to an iPhone that froze every few seconds ran smooth in testing. If a call misbehaves, please attach your log to an issue: it counts why frames drop, never what was said or shown. **Settings > Files > Log > Open** shows it, and the [guide](https://zapfast.rocks/settings-and-files/#the-log) says where it lives. By @crmne, building on the work of @alitura1, with the repair packets and pacing contributed to whatsapp-rust. (#255)
- **Group calls, as an experiment.** A group's header has the call buttons a person's has, a call to one of your groups rings here, and a call link in a message joins its call. Everyone hears everyone and each person gets a tile, but in testing your own video, as the others see it, still stopped for a moment every few seconds. By @crmne, building on the work of @alitura1. (#395)
- **Calls take the speaker's echo out of your microphone,** so the other side does not hear themselves when you call without headphones, all in Rust with WebRTC's echo canceller, noise suppression and gain control. By @crmne.
- **A Calls page and call history.** Every call takes its place in its chat, as on the phone: voice or video, who called, how long it lasted, or that it was missed or declined. Calls in the rail lists them newest first, with All and Missed chips, a search, and a call-back button on each row. New call offers the people you call most, then every contact. By @crmne, building on the work of @alitura1. (#256)
- **A navigation rail beside the chat list.** Chats and Calls sit at the top, and Starred, Archived, Locked chats, Settings and your own photo (the account switcher) at the bottom, on every platform. With the chat list hidden, your chats' pictures fill the rail. Ctrl+Shift+1 goes to Chats and Ctrl+Shift+2 to Calls (Command on a Mac). By @crmne.
- **ZapFast's sound talks to PipeWire directly on Linux,** else PulseAudio, else ALSA, on a new audio layer shared by voice messages, videos, call tones and notification sounds. It follows the default device when it changes and reopens it after a failure. ZapFast shows up as "ZapFast" in your volume mixer, and a call is marked as a call, so the system can lower other sound while it runs. By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- **Calls are a work in progress.** On a connection that loses many packets at once, the other side's picture can still stop for about a second until a fresh one arrives: the repair packets rebuild a few lost packets at a time, not a burst of ten or more. Sound and picture now carry the timing a phone needs to line them up, but they can still drift apart. In group calls your video stops for a moment every few seconds, since only one-to-one calls send repair packets yet. Please attach your log to an issue when a call misbehaves, and pull requests are very welcome: the call code is in `src/calls.rs` and in whatsapp-rust's `voip` module.
- The prebuilt Linux packages (tarball, AppImage, `.deb` and `.rpm`) need glibc 2.39: Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40 or newer. They do not run on Ubuntu 22.04 or Debian 12.
- Building ZapFast on Linux now needs libpipewire's headers and clang.
- On Windows a file pasted with Ctrl+V can still attach nothing for some people (#404). The log now says where it stopped; please attach it to that issue.
