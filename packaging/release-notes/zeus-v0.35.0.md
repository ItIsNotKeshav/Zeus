Zeus 0.35.0 is about calm and polish: the chat holds still while you scroll, everything opens and closes with the same smooth motion, and stickers and chat info got some love.

## New

- **Stickers fly into the chat.** A sticker you send from the picker glides into the conversation and stays visible on its way.
- **Chat info matches the chat card**, and you can drag photos, videos and files from it straight into another chat.
- **Smoother motion everywhere.** Popups, panels and menus now open and close with the same easing, toasts are calmer, and Settings has a slider for how strong the frosted glass is.
- **Frosted chat list header** that fades in as you scroll, with an accent coloured scroll bar in the chat list and the chat.
- **Unread counts** show the whole number instead of stopping at 99+.

## Fixed

- **The chat and the chat list no longer jump while you scroll.**
- The chat only follows new messages when you are already at the bottom, so reading older messages is not interrupted.
- Stickers that are a single frame animation now show up.
- Expired favourite stickers are set aside again instead of being asked for over and over.
- The full size picture in the viewer is let go once you move on or close it, which saves memory.
- Photos use less memory, and the media cache cleans itself up.
- The frosted glass no longer draws over a window that was closed and reopened.
- Certain broken video files can no longer freeze video or sticker playback.
- On macOS, the Dock icon is sharper when Zeus runs outside its app bundle.
