Zeus 0.20.0 gives the whole app a softer, rounder look, a new Settings page, and a quicker way to forward.

## New

- **A rounder, frosted look everywhere.** Menus, dialogs, cards and buttons have larger rounded corners, and menus and dialogs sit on frosted glass. The highlight around a selected message follows its corners.
- **Settings in the side list.** Settings categories now open in the left column, with the page itself on frosted glass and more room between rows.
- **Drag to forward.** Drag a message onto a chat in the list to forward it there, and drag photos and files out to Explorer or the desktop. The Forward dialog has a tidier layout.
- **Check for updates.** Settings has a Check for updates button, and Zeus asks you when an update is found.
- **Edit with the Up arrow.** Press Up in an empty message box to edit the last message you sent.
- **Reply and Edit first.** The right-click menu on a message now starts with Reply, then Edit.
